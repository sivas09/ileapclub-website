# iLEAP Member Portal — Facilitator & Center Director Guide

**Audience:** Facilitators and Center Directors

**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

This guide explains the main day-to-day tasks for supporting iLEAP Members. The options you see depend on your role and assignments.

## 1. Login and Dashboard

### Log in

1. Open the iLEAP Member Portal.
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. Select **Log In**.
4. Use the Dashboard and navigation menu to open Members, Meetings, and other areas.

### Change your password

1. Open your account or profile menu.
2. Select **Change Password**.
3. Enter your current password and your new password.
4. Save the change.

Facilitators see information for their assigned clubs. Center Directors see information for their assigned centres. Buttons for actions you cannot perform should not appear.

## 2. Role Permissions Summary

| Role | What the role can see |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | All centres, clubs, Members, and authorized portal information |
| Center Director | Only assigned centre or centres and the Members and clubs within them |
| Facilitator | Only assigned clubs and their Members |
| Member (Student role) | Only their own information and relevant club resources |

Always work within the access provided by the portal. If an expected Member, club, or meeting is missing, check assignments with an Admin.

## 3. Members

### For Facilitators

1. Open **Members** to see Members in your assigned clubs.
2. Use **View Details** to review a Member's information.
3. Select **Update Progress** to go directly to points, progress notes, and band requirements.
4. Select **Write Feedback** to add feedback.
5. Select **View Feedback** to review previous feedback.

### For Center Directors

1. Open **Members** to see Members in your assigned centre or centres.
2. Filter by centre or club when needed.
3. Use **Add Member** or **Edit Member** when those options are available.
4. Assign a Member only to a club within your assigned centre scope.
5. Review payment status from the Members area.

## 4. Meetings

1. Open **Meetings**.
2. Choose a meeting to view its date, details, roles, attendance, and other meeting tools.
3. If **Create Meeting** is available, select the correct club, enter the meeting details, and save.
4. Use **Book Roles** or the meeting role slots to assign available roles to Members.
5. Review assignments before the meeting.
6. Use **Download Agenda** when an agenda is needed.

You can work only with meetings inside your assigned club or centre scope.

## 5. Attendance

1. Open the meeting.
2. Open the **Attendance** section.
3. For each Member, choose **Present** or **Absent**.
4. Review the roster and select **Save Attendance**.
5. Look for the message **Attendance saved successfully.**

The roster includes active Members in that meeting's club. Members can see only their own attendance history.

## 6. Band Progress

1. Open **Members** and select **Update Progress** for the Member.
2. Find **Band Requirements**.
3. Review the Member's current program and band.
4. Mark a requirement complete when the Member has met it.
5. If a completion was entered by mistake, use the available undo or incomplete action to reverse it.
6. Save any changes when prompted.

Meeting **Personal Tracking** can also be used to select a Member and update progress where available.

Band completion confirms a specific learning requirement. Points are separate motivational rewards and do not complete band requirements.

## 7. Points and Progress Notes

1. Open **Members** and select **Update Progress** for the Member.
2. Review the Member's current **Total Points**.
3. Enter a positive number in **Add Points**.
4. Add a short **Reason** so the Member understands the award.
5. Select **Add Points** to save it.
6. Write or update **Progress Notes from Staff**.
7. Select **Save Progress Notes**.

Points are motivational and are not formal grades. Members can view their own total, recent point history, reasons, and staff progress notes. They cannot change them. Point deductions are not part of this simple workflow.

## 8. Feedback

1. Open **Members**.
2. Select **Write Feedback** beside the Member.
3. Enter clear, helpful feedback and save it.
4. Use **View Feedback** to read previous entries.

Staff feedback is written by authorized staff about a Member. **My Learning Reflection** is written by the Member about their own learning. Keep these separate.

## 9. My Learning Reflection

Members use **My Learning Reflection** to record:

- What they learned
- What they did well
- What they want to improve
- Whether they think they completed a band requirement

Authorized staff can view reflections for Members in their scope and may add a short response where the option is available. A Member's self-check means only “I think I completed this requirement.” It does not officially complete or sign off a band requirement.

## 10. Payments

### For Center Directors

1. Open **Members** and review the **Paid** or **Not Paid** status for Members in your assigned centre scope.
2. Change an individual Member's status when payment information is confirmed.
3. At the start of a new payment period, use the monthly reset action to set all active Members in your assigned centre scope to **Not Paid**.
4. Confirm the scope before using a bulk reset.

### For Facilitators

Payment status is not shown to Facilitators. Contact the Center Director or Admin if payment support is needed.

## 11. Documents and Resources

1. Open **Documents** or **Resources** from the portal navigation.
2. Select an item to view or download material relevant to your role and assignments.
3. If management controls are available, use them only for the intended centre or audience.
4. If you do not see an add, edit, or upload option, ask an Admin for help.

Members can access resources made available to them. Do not upload private Member information as a general resource.

## 12. Privacy and Security Rules

- Do not share your password or a Member's temporary password publicly.
- Use **Reset Password** only when needed, and share a temporary password securely.
- Access only Members inside your assigned club or centre scope.
- Do not download, copy, or share Member information unless it is needed for iLEAP work.
- Keep payment, progress, attendance, feedback, and reflection information private.
- Log out when you finish, especially on a shared device.

Facilitators cannot reset passwords. Admins can reset eligible user accounts, and an authorized Center Director can reset eligible Member or Facilitator accounts within their assigned centre scope.

## 13. Common Problems

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Cannot see a Member | Check that the Member is active and assigned to the correct club and centre. |
| Cannot see a meeting | Check that the meeting belongs to an assigned club. |
| Member forgot their password | Ask an Admin, or an authorized Center Director for an eligible in-scope account, to use **Reset Password**. |
| Member cannot see payment or progress | Check active membership, user account, centre, and club assignments. |
| A saved change is not visible | Refresh the page. If needed, use **Ctrl+F5** for a full refresh. |

If the problem continues, note the Member, club, screen, and error message before contacting the Admin.

## 14. Quick Checklist for Facilitators

- Check the meeting details.
- Mark attendance and save it.
- Confirm meeting role assignments.
- Add helpful feedback.
- Update band progress when a requirement is met.
- Add points and progress notes when appropriate.

## 15. Quick Checklist for Center Directors

- Review Members in the assigned centre or centres.
- Check payment status.
- Check upcoming meetings and assignments.
- Support Facilitators with member and club questions.
- Review progress, points, notes, and feedback.
- Ensure Members and Facilitators are assigned to the correct clubs.
